## Cottage cheese — new interest (2026-06-24)

Holger likes cottage cheese a lot. It's a **high-protein, low-fat** dairy (~24 g protein per pot for the *All Things* range, live cultures), so it fits the high-protein / low-fat goal squarely — and it's a **lean stand-in for cream / ricotta / silken-tofu** in sauces and bakes. Worth treating as a new pantry protein lever.

| # | Idea | Source | The gist → how to make it fit Holger | Status |
|--:|:-----|:-------|:-------------------------------------|:------:|
| 1 | **Butter Bean & Cottage Cheese Pesto Bowl** | [All Things Butter](https://allthingsbutter.co.uk/blogs/recipes/butter-beans-kale-salad) | Butter beans + cottage cheese + artichoke + massaged kale + a kale-basil-pine-nut pesto. **The protein/fibre engine.** Fit it: cut the pesto oil (4–5 → ~2 tbsp, loosen with lemon + bean-tin liquid), **nooch not parmesan**, add **chilli/harissa** for heat, and serve over **amaranth or rice** to turn a salad into a high-carb bowl. The lean parts (beans, cottage cheese, artichoke, kale) are low-fat; the pesto is the only fat to manage. **Best fit of the blog.** | 💡 |
| 2 | **Spinach–Cottage Cheese Herby Pancakes** | [All Things Butter](https://allthingsbutter.co.uk/blogs/recipes/spinach-herby-pancakes) | Cottage cheese + spinach + egg + herbs blitzed to a batter (only 60 g flour), pan-fried; topped with egg/seeds. Very cottage-cheese-forward, high protein, lean batter. Fit it: easy on the "knob of butter", **bulk with a carb side**, add chilli. Lower-carb and not spicy as written — a good high-protein breakfast, secondary to #1. | 💡 |
| 3 | **Cottage cheese as the silken-tofu-cream replacement** | own idea | Blend cottage cheese smooth and fold it in **off the heat** as the creamy finish in a curry/soup — higher protein than the silken-tofu cream (e.g. the coconut–tomato curry's tofu-cream role). ⚠ Lara's "tofu must be fully blended" condition applies to cottage cheese too — blend it, don't leave curds. Test against the silken-tofu version. | 💡 |
