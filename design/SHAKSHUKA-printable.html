* Indian: Tikka or Madras, not both; bold runs tikka 9 g. Hold tikka above cumin/mustard so it reads tikka, not cumin.

⚠ Solo-only: smoked paprika (Lara dislikes smoke — sweet paprika fine) & red miso (Jannes dislikes funk — white miso is family default).

Umami rule: every tomato-base profile bhunas the tomato paste — except mole, whose umami is aminos + nooch + shiitake (glutamate × guanylate). Soaked chillies: blend the soak water in (half the flavour). Stir shiitake in with the passata in Thai (it scorches dry).

Kid-safe Mexican: 300 g passata + 50 g grated carrot, all chilli at the table. Bhuna fix when flat: deglaze the stuck fond with water splashes, repeat 2–3× — the fond is concentrated flavour, not a burn.

Key: amounts per single serving, g unless noted · tbl table · pc whole dried chilli · — not used · ⚠ family-palate caveat.
