## Post-Workout Cream

Post-run fuelling. Tofu + fruit + dates + texture-modifier. Bridges from end-of-run to lunch without spiking blood sugar.

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|:-----------|-------:|:------|
| 🫘 Silken tofu | 300 g | Protein base; blends to a smooth dessert-like texture |
| 🍑 Peach slices in juice | 210 g | Drained; juice can be reserved for sweetness adjustment |
| 🌴 Medjool dates | 25 g | Pitted, torn small before blending |
| ⚫ Cacao nibs *or* 🌾 chia seeds | 15 g | Texture / delayed gastric emptying — see notes |

**Method:** Blend tofu + peach + dates until smooth. Stir in cacao nibs or chia seeds at the end (don't blend — keep the texture intact for the gastric-emptying effect). Serve immediately or chill.

**Nutrition (approx, whole recipe):**

| Macro | Total |
|:------|------:|
| Energy | ~463 kcal |
| Protein | ~23 g |
| Carbohydrates | ~57 g |
| Fat | ~13 g |

(Provenance: outgoing-Claude memory; not FoodNoms-verified. Replace with FoodNoms totals next cook.)

**Notes:**

- **Delayed gastric emptying is intentional, not a flaw.** Cacao nibs and chia seeds both slow digestion — this is the bridge to lunch the recipe is designed for. Do not substitute with faster-digesting alternatives without understanding why.
- Cacao nibs and chia are interchangeable for the texture/timing function. Cacao nibs add bitterness and crunch; chia gels slightly in the mouth.
- This is a recovery snack, not a meal. Don't scale up to family-portion size.
- Compatible with the 8 pm cutoff if run timing allows; otherwise treat as mid-afternoon.

## Date & Quinoa-Flake Energy Bites

No-bake running fuel — a **pantry run-down** for the quinoa flakes *and* the chopped dates (both need eating before the move). Date paste is the binder (no honey), defatted peanut flour the protein, toasted quinoa flakes the carb body. Low-fat by design so they sit light before a run. Makes **~12 bites**.

| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|:-----------|-------:|:------|
| 🌾 Quinoa flakes (toasted) | 100 g | the run-down; toast for a nuttier flavour |
| 🌴 Chopped dates → paste | 120 g | **primary binder** — soak + mash (see method); no honey |
| 🥜 Defatted peanut flour | 50 g | protein, near fat-free |
| 🥛 Semi-skimmed milk | 30 ml | to loosen, as needed |
| 🧂 Salt | 1 g | pinch — electrolytes (welcome for a runner) |
| 💧 Date soak water | ~40 ml | absorbed into the paste (0 kcal) |

*Optional stocked add-in (omitted from the nutrition): ⚫ cacao nibs ~15 g for chocolate crunch — another run-down — or a pinch of cinnamon. Stir in at step 3.*

**Method:**

1. **Date paste.** Cover the 🌴 chopped dates with just-boiled water; soak **10–15 min** (or microwave with a splash of water 30–60 s). Drain, **keeping the water**. Mash/blend to a thick, peanut-butter-consistency paste, adding the reserved water a spoonful at a time — stop while it's still stiff.
2. **Toast the flakes.** Dry pan or air fryer ~160–180 °C a few minutes until golden and nutty (optional, but worth it). Cool.
3. **Mix.** Combine 🌾 flakes + 🥜 peanut flour + 🧂 salt, then work in the date paste until it forms a stiff dough. Crumbly → splash in 🥛 milk (or the soak water); too wet → more flakes. (Add ⚫ cacao nibs here if using.)
4. **Roll** into ~12 walnut-sized bites (~28 g each). **Chill 30 min+** to firm up.
5. Keeps in the fridge ~1 week, or freeze.

**Pre-run use:** 1–2 bites is a clean ~75–150 kcal quick-carb hit — low fat, modest fibre per bite, settles fast. (For the *very-early-before-a-10k* slot, semolina pudding is still the gentler first choice; these are the grab-and-go option and the all-day training snack.)

**Notes:**

- **No honey — date paste does all the binding.** Dates are stickier than honey and bring fibre + whole-food carbs; this also depletes *two* run-down items at once (flakes + dates).
- **Toast the flakes** for flavour, but they also bind better slightly toasted (less raw-starch chalkiness).
- **Texture knobs:** more date paste = chewier and sweeter; more peanut flour = drier, higher protein; a splash more milk = softer. If they won't hold, the paste was too dry — loosen it and re-mix.
- **Fat is low (~1 g/bite)** because the peanut flour is defatted — that's what keeps them gut-friendly around running. Cacao nibs add fat (~10 g per 15 g) if you include them.
- USDA records: quinoa = "Quinoa, uncooked" (`168874`) — quinoa flakes are just rolled raw quinoa, same composition; dates = "Dates, medjool" (`168191`). Peanut flour + milk from Holger's own label records.
