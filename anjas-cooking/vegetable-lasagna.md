**3635 g in → 2536 g out, a 30.2% loss.** That is 3255 g of ingredients plus the 380 g of water. ⚠ **It is a composite**: the ragù reduces on the hob, the sheets absorb liquid from it, and the assembled dish evaporates in the oven. The three are not separable from one weighing.

⚠ **Read as a NET weight.** Holger says "in tray" or "in pot" when a reading includes the vessel — the tagine, the millet and the Zwiebelkuchen v3 all did — and this one did not. **Arithmetic cannot settle it here**: 2536 g net gives a 30.2% loss, while 2536 g gross less a ~654 g ceramic tray would give 1882 g and a 48% loss, and both are possible for a bake. The reading rests on the wording, not on the numbers.

## Provenance notes

- ⚠⚠ **"2 cans" had no weight and 800 g is an assumption**, taken at 400 g a can on the precedent of Anja's tagine four days earlier. **At 800 g it is a quarter of the dish by mass**, so this is a bigger assumption than the usual brand guess — if the cans were 400 g drained or a different size, the figure moves materially. The **Cirio** brand is also assumed from the same precedent.
- ⚠ **The lasagna sheets are costed against the La Molisana wholewheat durum pasta record** — no lasagna sheet record exists. Both are dry wholewheat durum, so the proxy is close, but **the sheets are 36% of the dish's energy**, which makes it the most load-bearing proxy in the file.
- ⚠ **Lurpak is costed against generic salted butter** (`173410`). Lurpak is a cultured butter with a slightly different fat and water balance, immaterial at 42 g.
- ⚠ **The semi-skimmed milk record is the one carrying the retired UK milk.co.uk reference.** That now affects three dishes — the Kaiserschmarren of 08-11, the quinoa bake of 13-11 and this one — and it is still an open item. **A photograph of the carton actually in the fridge would close it.**
- **No cheese.** Unusual for a lasagna and worth recording as a fact of the dish rather than an omission: the richness is butter, milk and walnuts. The 1205 mg of calcium is milk and vegetables, not mozzarella.
