## Notes

- **🍶 White miso and 🧀 nooch together, which is the pairing the project keeps arriving at** — fermented depth plus the cheesy note. The same two did the work in the squash quiche and, with brown miso, in the Cheesy Roasted Cauliflower Soup two days earlier.
- **🍋 5 g of lemon juice in the bake rather than at the table.** A trivial dose and the only in-dish acid, which is unusual for this kitchen — the standing default (observation 60) is acid at the table. At 5 g in 1.6 kg it is seasoning, not an acid component.
- ⚠ **🥦 Broccoli and 🥬 cauliflower both baked**, which is the one thing that may cost it: prolonged heat is what makes brassicas sulfurous, and there are 597 g of them here. Worth asking whether it read cabbagey.
- **🍠 67 g of roasted purple sweet potato and 302 g of cauliflower are both leftovers** — from the 10-11 bowls and the 11-11 soup respectively. A genuinely efficient use of two dishes' remainders.
