**The headline difference is water, not vegetables.** v1 carried ~2344 g of water in the finished pot against v2's ~874 g, from a *larger* solids load. **v2 is a substantially thicker soup** — and because nutrition comes from the solids, the same arithmetic spread over 25% less liquid makes it considerably denser per spoonful.

**One real ingredient change: 🧅 leek became onion**, with 32 g of 🌱 spring onion alongside. Everything else is the same vegetables in slightly different amounts, plus 13 g of 🥑 oil where v1 listed no added fat at all.
