# Books — Reference Material Index

Booklets and ebooks Holger bought (audiobook PDFs, digital booklets, scanned
volumes). This index is the **map**: what each book is, how it's structured,
and — for a vegetarian family in pantry-run-down mode — **when to reach for
it**. Claude should consult these and cite them by page when relevant, the same
way `docs/Nussinow_Cooking_Times.md` is used for pressure-cooking.

> **These are reference, not recipe files.** They are not transcribed in full
> (the volumes are far too large). This index points into them; open the PDF to
> the cited page for the detail.

---

## 1. The Flavor Equation — Nik Sharma (illustrated booklet)

*`Digital Booklet - The Flavor Equation …Unabridged.pdf`* · 134 pages · cite by **booklet page (PDF page)**.

The audiobook's **illustrated companion** — the book's science diagrams and its
100+ recipes, one chart/recipe per spread. This is the single most relevant
book for our **flavour-profile work** (shakshuka profiles, the umami/EUC
discussions, tamarind sourness). Its whole architecture *is* the framework
we've been using informally: flavour built from **7 elements**.

**Front science charts** (booklet p1–15):
- Cultural/regional flavour pairing networks (p3) · Emotion & taste (p5)
- **Table of common food pigments** + pH colour behaviour (p6) · taste of colours/shapes (p7)
- Egg protein chart (p8) · texture boosters (p9) · aromas by chemical structure (p10)
- Extracting flavour by heat (p11) · how taste buds work (p12)
- **Anatomy of a recipe** (p14) · **Flavour map of a stock** (p15)

**The 7 flavour elements** — each opens with a "Flavor Approach" page + recipes:
| Element | Starts | Key chart | Relevance here |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Brightness** (acidity) | p16 | pH scale (p17); onion colour/texture (p18); pomegranate (p19) | tamarind/lemon/sumac finishes; the acid-balance calls |
| **Bitterness** | p34 | — | reishi/mushroom-blend, cacao in mole |
| **Saltiness** | p48 | — | the salt↔umami distinction we keep hitting |
| **Sweetness** | p61 | natural sweetness boosters (p62) | carrot-in-Mexican, squash sweetness |
| **Savoriness** (umami) | p77 | **nutritional yeast (p78)** | the whole umami/shiitake/nooch programme |
| **Fieriness** (heat) | p89 | **chilli heat scale (p90)** | chilli-at-table decisions, chipotle/ancho |
| **Richness** (fat) | p106 | grapeseed oil (p107) | coconut-milk/oil finishes |

**Pantry** (p122): spice blends (p123) · cured yolks (p124) · dipping oils (p125) · **preserved lemons two ways (p127)**.
**End reference charts** (p131–134): pure water in the kitchen · cooking fats & oils · grain vs root/waxy starches.

**Reach for it when:** designing or debugging a flavour profile; deciding an acid
or umami move; wanting the element vocabulary (brightness/savoriness/…) to reason
about a dish. Nik Sharma is South-Indian-leaning — good for the tamarind/curry-powder direction.

## 2. Modernist Cuisine, Volume 2: Techniques and Equipment — Myhrvold et al.

*`modernist-cuisine-volume-2_compress.pdf`* · 487 PDF pages · cite by **book page (PDF page = book + 10)**.

The scanned volume. Its front matter (PDF p8–11) prints the **whole six-volume
master TOC** with **per-volume** page numbers — don't be fooled: only the
Volume 2 chapters below are actually in this file. (E.g. "Ch 12 Plant Foods →
Pressure-Cooking p298" is **Volume 3**, *not here*.)

**Chapter 7 — Traditional Cooking** (book p2 / PDF p12):
Grilling 7 · Broiling 18 · Roasting 28 · Panfrying à la plancha 37 · **Sautéing 44** ·
Stir-Frying 48 · Covered Sautéing 58 · **Boiling 63** · **Steaming 70** · **Canning 75** ·
**Pot-Roasting and Stewing 93** · **Baking 101** · Cooking in Oil 115 · Smoking 132.

**Chapter 8 — Cooking in Modern Ovens** (book p150 / PDF p160):
Cooking with Moist Air 154 · **Cooking with Microwaves 182**.

**Chapter 9 — Cooking Sous Vide** (book p192 / PDF p202):
Why Sous Vide 198 · Packaging 208 · Equipment 228 · Strategies for Cooking 242 ·
Chilling & Reheating 252 · Blanching & Searing 267.

**Chapter 10 — The Modernist Kitchen** (book p280 / PDF p290):
**Extracting Flavors 288** · Infusing Essences 318 · Juicing 332 · Filtering 351 ·
**Concentrating 379** · Cutting Down to Size 398 · **Drying 428** · Cryogenic Freezing & Carbonating 456.

**Pressure cooking** is discussed *incidentally* across Ch 7's boiling/steaming/
stewing/canning cluster (≈ book p85–97 / PDF p95–107), not as a dedicated
section — for pressure-cook times stay with **Nussinow**; this adds the *why*.

**Reach for it when:** you want the science behind a technique already in our
kit — **the microwave-onion jumpstart** (Ch 8 Microwaves), steaming/boiling veg,
baking, or **flavour extraction/concentration/drying** for stocks, purées and
powders (Ch 10, relevant to the blender + shiitake-powder work). Meat/seafood
(Ch 11+) is Volume 3 and absent.

## 3. Cooked: A Natural History of Transformation — Michael Pollan (audiobook booklet)

*`Digital Booklet.pdf`* · 13 pages · cite by **PDF page**.

The audiobook's appendices only (not the full text). Framed around Pollan's
**four transformations** — Fire, Water, Air, Earth.

**Appendix I — Four Recipes:**
1. **Fire** — Pork Shoulder Barbecue (+ an Asian dipping sauce built on **dashi**) — p3–5
2. **Water** — Meat Sugo and Pasta — p5–7
3. **Air** — **Whole-Wheat Country Loaf** (sourdough: starter → leaven → bulk ferment → shape → proof → bake) — p7–10
4. **Earth** — **Sauerkraut** — p10–11

**Appendix II — A Short Shelf of Books on Cooking** (annotated bibliography, grouped Fire/Water/Air/Earth) — p12–13.

**Reach for it when:** the **sourdough loaf** or **sauerkraut** method is wanted
(Earth ties directly to the **sauerkraut hash** in `design/SHAKSHUKA.md`); or for
the four-transformations mental model. Note: recipes 1–2 are **meat** (pork, meat
sugo) — of limited direct use for this family; the dashi technique and the
bread/kraut are the takeaways.
