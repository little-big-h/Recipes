# Creamy Pinto & Butternut Soup — Bold (v2, no corn)

*A bolder reworking of the pinto–butternut soup: corn dropped, the cumin–paprika–garlic backbone cranked, and a bloomed **tomato-paste umami anchor** (the fix for v1's blandness) — plus more shiitake, white miso and kombu for a deep savoury base that doesn't need table-rescuing. **White** miso (not red) keeps it Jannes-friendlier; the boldness comes from the spice + tomato + umami stack, not fermentation. Steamed cauliflower on top for contrast. Heat stays mild in the pot — lime and rehydrated bird's-eye chilli at the table for the adults. Part-puréed (⅔ blended, ⅓ whole) so the pinto registers. Family of five.*

## Method

> **Note:** Peel and cube 🎃 butternut ~3 cm. Trim 🌼 cauliflower into florets. Wipe 🌊 kombu (don't rinse). Measure the spices. Optionally rehydrate a 🌶️ bird's-eye chilli in just-boiled water for the table.

1. **Phase 1 — squash.** Put 🎃 butternut, 🧅 dried onion, 🍄 shiitake powder, 🌊 kombu and 💧 1600 ml water in the Ninja (no basket yet). Seal — low pressure, 30 min, natural release. (With the corn gone this just softens the squash and infuses the aromatics — you can drop it to ~10 min if you're in a hurry.)

2. **Phase 2 — beans.** Open the Ninja and **lift out the 🌊 kombu** (umami's in the broth; pulling it now limits iodine/bitterness). Set the rack in, put 🫘 pinto beans in the basket, lower into the hot liquid. Seal — high pressure, 20 min, natural release.

3. During the phase-2 release: heat 🥑 avocado oil in the wok. Bloom 🌰 cumin, 🌿 coriander, 🫑 sweet paprika and 🧄 garlic powder ~30 sec with a splash of cooking liquid, then **add 🥫 tomato paste and fry ~1 min until it darkens** — this is the umami depth v1 was missing. Set aside.

4. Steam 🌼 cauliflower 8–10 min until crisp-tender (it's denser than broccoli — give it the extra couple of minutes). Hold warm — it goes on top.

5. Lift out the basket of 🫘 beans. Set aside **about one-third whole**. Blend the 🎃 squash, cooking liquid, the remaining **two-thirds of the beans** and the bloomed 🌰 spice-and-tomato mix until completely smooth. Thin with more cooking liquid/water to taste — keep it loose.

6. Return to low heat; stir the whole 🫘 beans back in to warm. **Off the heat, whisk in 🫙 white miso** (never boil). Season with 🫗 liquid aminos, then 🧂 salt and black pepper to taste — **taste boldly** (target ~12 g total salt; the tomato/shiitake/miso stack should already read deep and savoury). Do not hard-boil.

7. Ladle into bowls. Pile steamed 🌼 cauliflower on top. 🍋 Lime and the rehydrated 🌶️ chilli at the table for the adults.

## Design notes

- **What changed from v1 (a bolder reformulation, several variables).** Corn dropped; **white** miso for red; **cauliflower** for broccoli; and the seasoning cranked — cumin 6→12 g, sweet paprika 8→12 g, garlic powder 5→8 g, plus new **ground coriander** and the **bloomed tomato paste** umami anchor, with shiitake 8→12 g and white miso bumped. Butternut bumped 1000→1200 g to fill the volume corn left.
- **Tomato paste is the bold-savoury engine.** v1 went flat because the sweet base had no umami anchor once the kombu was pulled; bhuna'd tomato paste (plus more shiitake + white miso) fixes that at the root — the boldness is *savoury depth*, not just more dry spice.
- **Not the clean miso test.** This changes too many things at once to read as the white-vs-red miso experiment (`../../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md` #4) — keep that as a separate single-variable cook.
- **Jannes / cauliflower flag.** Steamed on top is the *most visible* form of his one flagged vegetable dislike. If his rating drops, blend the cauliflower into the base next time (it purées smooth and vanishes) rather than topping with it.
- **Heat.** Pot stays mild for the kids; the rehydrated bird's-eye chilli is an at-table adult option. Sweet (not smoked) paprika keeps it Lara-safe.
- **Two-phase still earns its place** — it protects the whole-bean third. With corn gone, phase 1 no longer needs the full 30 min (it was for corn sweetness); ~10 min suffices to soften the squash.
- **Run-down note:** uses only in-stock spices/pastes (cumin, coriander, paprika, garlic powder, tomato paste, shiitake, white miso, kombu) — no new buys.
