# Creamy Corn Soup — Mexican Profile (2 kg)

*Golden blended corn soup with chipotle, cumin, paprika, coriander and oregano. Asparagus steamed separately once pressure is reached. Red kidney beans for contrast. Finished with spinach, tamarind and lime. Served with capers. Family of five + leftovers.*

> **Migrated from legacy PDF source** (`creamy-corn-soup-mexican-2kg.pdf`). No family ratings on record.

> ⚠️ **Lara-conflict flag:** 20 g smoked paprika is substantial — Lara concern. For a Lara-friendlier version, drop the smoked paprika and double the dried coriander + oregano to compensate. The chipotle is already removed before blending (no chili in final soup).

> **Scaled-down variant** (1 kg corn, family of 5 no leftovers): halve all amounts. **Technique difference at the smaller scale:** the original 1 kg recipe simmered asparagus IN the soup (5–8 min, switch to sauté mode after blending) rather than steaming separately. Either approach works; steaming separately gives cleaner asparagus colour and texture, simmering-in extracts more asparagus flavour into the broth.

> **Bean form note:** the corn-soup matrix in `../../design/CORN-SOUPS.md` specifies dried red kidney beans 250 g. This recipe uses **4 × 400 g tinned kidney beans (~960 g drained)** — a substantially different bean form and quantity. The matrix represents design intent; the recipe represents what was actually cooked.

## Method

1. (0–10 min) Allow 🌽 frozen corn to partially thaw at room temperature. Dice 🧅 onions, roughly chop 🧄 garlic.

2. (10–18 min) Sauté mode on Ninja: cook 🧅 onions and 🧄 garlic dry 6–8 minutes until softened.

3. (~18 min) Add 🌱 cumin seeds, 🫑 smoked paprika, 🌿 dried coriander and 🪴 oregano — bloom 1–2 minutes.

4. (~20 min) Add 🌽 corn, whole 🌶️ chipotles and 🧊 stock. Seal — high pressure, 10 min, delayed release (10 min). **Heat-up ~30 min** (2 kg partially frozen load).

5. (~50 min, once pressure is reached) Start steaming 🥦 asparagus in bamboo steamers (8–10 min). They can sit and wait if ready early.

6. (~70 min, after release) Remove all 4 🌶️ chipotles — set aside to eat separately. Stir in ✨ nutritional yeast. Blend soup until completely smooth. Strain through a sieve for extra silkiness if desired.

7. (~77 min) Add rinsed 🫘 kidney beans to soup. Warm through 3 minutes on sauté mode.

8. (~80 min) Off heat: stir in 🥬 spinach until wilted. Stir in 🥭 tamarind paste and 🍋 lime juice. Taste and adjust 🧂 salt.

9. Serve in bowls, 🥦 asparagus alongside, 🫒 capers scattered over.
