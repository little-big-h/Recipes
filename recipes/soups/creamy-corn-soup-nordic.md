# Creamy Corn Soup — Nordic Profile

*Sweet corn and black-eyed bean soup, chilled and finished with quark and fresh dill. Served with classic Nordic pickled accompaniments. Make the day before — needs at least 4 hours chilling, overnight is better. Family of five.*

> **Make ahead:** cook today, fold in quark and dill just before serving tomorrow. Cold mutes flavour — season more generously than you would for a hot soup, and add lemon juice after chilling.

## Method

> **Note:** Allow 🌽 corn to partially thaw before loading. No soaking needed for 🫘 black-eyed beans.

1. Add 🧅 onion, 🍃 bay leaves, 🧊 2 stock cubes, 2000 ml water and 🌽 corn to Ninja. Seal — low pressure, 30 min, natural release.

2. Once fully released (at about 100 minutes): place rack in Ninja. Put 🫘 black-eyed beans in steaming basket and set on rack in the corn liquid. Seal — low pressure, 8 min, natural release.

3. Once fully released (at about 138 minutes): lift out basket of 🫘 beans and set aside. Remove 🍃 bay leaves. Blend 🌽 corn completely smooth — blend thoroughly for a creamy texture.

4. Stir drained 🫘 black-eyed beans into the blended soup. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.

5. Once fully chilled: whisk in 🫙 quark and fold in 🌿 fresh dill. Add 🍋 lemon juice generously — cold mutes acidity. Season well with 🧂 salt and white pepper. Taste and adjust.

6. Serve in chilled bowls, topped with 🌱 chives and extra 🌿 dill fronds. Accompany with 🥒 pickled cucumber, 🫐 pickled beetroot, 🪻 pickled red onion and 🫚 freshly grated horseradish on the side.
