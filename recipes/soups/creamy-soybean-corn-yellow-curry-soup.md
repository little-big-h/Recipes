# Creamy Yellow Curry Soy Bean & Corn Soup [07-07-26] ✴️

*A creamy soup built on pureed soy beans — most of the batch blended smooth with canned corn, Biona light coconut milk and yellow Thai curry paste, a third of the beans left whole for texture. Peanut flour (moderate — Anja isn't a big fan) is whisked into the coconut milk for extra body without turning it into a peanut dish. Cauliflower is air-fried separately, then folded in late just to heat through — coated in a bold savoury base rather than left visible, the presentation that has consistently worked for Jannes's cauliflower aversion. White miso finishes it off the heat. No acid in the pot: tamarind paste is offered at the table, in tamarind's usual role for a West African/Mexican/Indian-leaning profile, rather than lime. Family of five.*

## Method

> **Prep:** Cut 🌼 cauliflower into florets. Drain the 🌽 canned corn. Whisk the 🥜 peanut flour into the 🥥 coconut milk until smooth (prevents clumping — see `../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md`). Wash the 🥬 spinach. Measure the 🍛 curry paste and 🍶 miso.

1. **Air-fry the cauliflower.** Toss the 🌼 florets in the 🥑 avocado oil and air-fry at 200C for 18-20 min, shaking halfway, until golden and tender. Set aside — it goes back in near the end just to heat through.

2. **Pressure-cook the soybeans.** Load the 🫘 dry soybeans and 💧 water into the Ninja. Seal — high pressure, 40 min, natural release. No soak needed; this runs unattended while you're out picking up Jannes.

3. **Bloom the curry paste.** Once the beans are done, ladle a little of the hot cooking liquid into a pan with the 🍛 yellow curry paste and fry 1-2 min until fragrant, splashing in more liquid if it catches.

4. **Set aside a third of the beans.** Lift out about one-third of the cooked 🫘 soybeans with a slotted spoon and set aside whole, so they register as texture rather than vanishing into the puree.

5. **Blend the base.** In the blender, combine the remaining two-thirds of the 🫘 soybeans, the cooking liquid, the bloomed 🍛 curry paste, the drained 🌽 corn, and the 🥥 coconut milk-🥜 peanut flour mixture. Blend until completely smooth.

6. **Warm through.** Return the puree to the pot over low heat. Stir the reserved whole 🫘 beans and the air-fried 🌼 cauliflower back in to warm through, about 5 min.

7. **Wilt the spinach.** Stir in the 🥬 spinach and cook just until it collapses, about 2 min.

8. **Finish off heat.** Pull off the heat and whisk in the 🍶 white miso (never boil). Taste and adjust.

9. **Serve.** Ladle into bowls. 🫙 Tamarind paste, diluted in a splash of hot water, at the table for those who want acidity — kept off the main pot per the family's acid-at-the-table convention.

## Design notes

- **Blend most, keep some whole — texture, not a repeat of the earlier soy-bean soup's problem.** The first Creamy Butternut & Soy Bean Soup cratered with the kids (`../../docs/RATINGS.md` remark 38), but the corrected read (obs 18) is that a **bitter kombu-shiitake dashi** was the cause, not the soy beans themselves. This recipe has no dashi/kombu at all, so keeping a third of the beans whole for texture — the same part-purée pattern as the Pinto-Butternut soup — should be safe.
- **Single combined appliance, sequential not parallel.** The Ninja ML750 air-fries and pressure-cooks, but not at once — cauliflower goes in and out first (fast, low-attention), then the pot is reloaded with beans and sealed for the long unattended pressure-cook. Plan the school-pickup window around the *pressure-cook* stage, not the air-fry stage.
- **Cauliflower folded in, not left visible.** Per the established pattern (obs 19: pinto v2, the tikka curry), Jannes's cauliflower aversion has repeatedly not fired when it's coated in a bold savoury base rather than served naked/visible. Folding it into the curry-peanut-miso puree (rather than piling it on top) leans into that.
- **Peanut flour kept moderate and pre-mixed.** 40 g in a ~2.8 kg pot (~1.4%) is a supporting note, not a peanut-forward dish — Anja isn't a big fan and Julina has skipped peanut dressings before. Whisking it into the coconut milk first avoids the clumping TECHNIQUES.md warns about.
- **Tamarind instead of lime, still at the table.** Tamarind is in its usual stocked role (West African/Mexican/Indian-leaning acid) rather than the more common Thai-curry lime — but it's still kept off the main pot, per the standing rule that acid stays at the table for Lara.
- **Pantry run-down.** Every ingredient here — dry soybeans, canned corn, yellow curry paste, peanut flour, tamarind, white miso, coconut milk — is a stocked staple; nothing bought for this one.
- **Untested combination.** This is a new flavour direction for the project (soy-bean base + Thai curry + peanut + tamarind, no squash) — no prior data point to predict against. Worth watching Anja (peanut) and Jannes (cauliflower, even folded in) specifically on first cook.
