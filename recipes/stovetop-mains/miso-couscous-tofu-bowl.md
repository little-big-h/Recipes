# Miso Couscous Bowl with Air-Fried Tofu

*A single-serving **carb-loading** bowl (Holger solo, pre-training): an Itsu-style 🍶 miso broth deepened with 🍄 shiitake, 🥣 tsuyu and 🫗 aminos, poured over 🌾 couscous with 🧊 air-fried tofu for texture and 🍙 nori flakes on top. Built to a **low-fat / moderate-fibre / high-carb** brief — ~86 g carb, ~10 g fat, most of that fat from the tofu. 🌾 Couscous over noodles or millet on purpose: refined semolina is the lowest-residue, fastest, near-zero-fat carb of the options. 🌶️ Shichimi at the table for heat.*

## Method

1. **Soak the couscous.** 🌾 Couscous in a bowl, pour over just-boiled 💧 water to cover (~135 ml), add a couple of drops of 🫗 aminos so the base is seasoned through. Lid on, 5 min, then fluff with a fork.
2. **Air-fry the tofu** (if not already done): cube the 🧊 firm tofu, air-fry **200 °C, ~12 min**, until golden and firm. *(Often already made — just warm through.)*
3. **Build the broth.** In a small pan, warm ~350 ml 💧 water with the 🥣 tsuyu, 🫗 aminos, 🍄 shiitake powder and a pinch of 🫚 dried ginger. Add the 🍯 hon-mirin and let it simmer ~30 sec to cook off the alcohol (nikiri).
4. **Miso off heat.** Take the pan off the heat, then whisk in the 🍶 white miso until smooth — **never boil miso**. Finish with a few drops of 🫙 rice vinegar to lift it.
5. **Assemble.** 🌾 Couscous and 🧊 tofu in the bowl, pour the hot broth over, scatter 🍙 nori flakes on top.
6. **At the table:** 🌶️ shichimi to taste.

## Design notes

- **Carb-load brief drove every choice: low-fat, moderate-fibre, high-carb.** The base is ~86 g carb with almost no fat outside the tofu — the fast-digesting, low-residue plate you want the night before hard training.
- **Couscous over soba or millet — on purpose.** Refined semolina is the **lowest-residue** and lowest-fat of the three, cooks by just soaking, and hits the highest usable-carb density. Soba would've been the "en-cuisine" match with miso but carries more fibre and often added salt; millet is a whole grain (more residue, slightly more fat, 20-min cook). Authenticity wasn't the goal — function was.
- **Umami without kombu (out of stock): 🍄 shiitake × 🍶 miso.** Guanylate × glutamate is the multiplicative synergy that makes a dashi punch; 🥣 tsuyu + 🫗 aminos add the soy/dashi backbone. This is the same lever documented across the shakshuka line.
- **🍙 Nori is a ~10-kcal flavour bomb — but 41 % fibre.** Calorie-trivial, so the constraint isn't energy, it's fibre: a 5 g garnish adds ~2 g fibre and keeps the bowl *moderate*; melting a big handful in (tempting, given the BWFO tub needs running down) would push it out of low-residue territory. Garnish, don't dose.
- **Miso off the heat, always.** Whisked in after the pan comes off — boiling miso dulls it.
- **Tofu is the one fat source** (~9 g of the ~10 g total). Scale it down for a leaner load, or keep it for the protein + texture.
- **Salt ~2.9 g is a feature here** — electrolyte pre-loading for endurance training, consistent with Holger's standing use of salt as a training lever, not a seasoning fault.
- **Solo dish.** Holger's own carb-load dinner, single serving — no family panel.
