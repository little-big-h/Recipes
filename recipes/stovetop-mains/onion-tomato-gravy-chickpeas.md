# Smooth Onion–Tomato Gravy with Chickpeas

*A versatile, silky onion–tomato base: **microwave-jumpstarted onions** sautéed to deep gold, **bhuna'd tomato paste**, then **two tins (800 g) of hand-crushed whole tomatoes** simmered down and **blended smooth** — folded with chickpeas and finished off-heat with white miso + blended cottage cheese for a high-protein, low-fat **makhani-style** richness. Smooth for Lara, mild for Jannes (chilli + lemon at the table), high-protein and very-high-fibre for Holger. The blended gravy (before the chickpeas) is a **reusable base** — swap in tofu, paneer, butternut or kofta. Family of five; serve over basmati.*

## Method

> **Prep.** Drain the 🫘 chickpeas. **Hand-crush** the 🥫 whole tomatoes (or they'll break up unevenly in the simmer). Blitz the 🧀 cottage cheese smooth ahead of the finish — Lara: no visible curds.

1. **Microwave the onions.** Chopped 🧅 onion in a bowl, microwave **~5–6 min** until soft and translucent — this collapses the cells so they caramelise in about half the pan time, with less oil.
2. **Sauté.** Heat the 🥑 oil in the wok, add the microwaved 🧅 onion, and sauté **~8–10 min** to deep golden (fast now that it's pre-softened).
3. **Bhuna the paste.** Push the onions aside, add the 🍅 tomato paste, and fry **~2 min** until it darkens — the umami anchor.
4. **Aromatics + spices.** Add 🧄 garlic + 🫚 ginger, then 🌰 cumin, 🌿 coriander, 🌼 turmeric, 🍛 garam masala, 🫑 paprika; bloom **~1 min**.
5. **Tomatoes + simmer.** Add the hand-crushed 🥫 canned tomatoes; simmer **15–20 min**, reducing to a **thick, jammy** gravy — whole tomatoes are juicy, so reduce them down properly. Salt to taste.
6. **Blend smooth.** Cool slightly, then blend the gravy until silky in the standalone blender (vent the lid for hot liquid). This is the smooth-gravy step — ideal for Lara.
7. **Chickpeas.** Return the gravy to the pan, fold in the 🫘 chickpeas, and simmer **5–10 min** to meld; loosen with a splash of water to a pourable gravy.
8. **Finish off heat.** Whisk in the 🍶 white miso + the blended 🧀 cottage cheese for a makhani-style creamy, high-protein finish. **Never boil after the dairy/miso** (it splits / the miso goes flat).
9. **Serve.** 🍃 fresh coriander over; 🌶️ chilli + 🍋 lemon at the table. Over basmati rice.

## Design notes

- **Microwave-then-sauté onions** (Holger's move). Pre-softening in the microwave collapses the onion cells, so they hit deep gold in roughly half the pan time and with less oil — a real shortcut for an onion-heavy base.
- **Whole canned tomatoes, hand-crushed → reduced → blended.** Better fruit than passata and no calcium-chloride firming, so they melt silky. They're juicy, so **reduce hard** before blending (the watery-base failure mode). The acid-stall rule isn't in play here — the chickpeas are pre-cooked (tinned) and go in *after* the tomatoes, so there's nothing for the acid to toughen.
- **Blend-smooth = Lara-friendly.** A silky gravy with no recognisable pieces is exactly her texture; folding the chickpeas in *after* the blend keeps some body. For a fully smooth bowl, blend a ladle of the chickpeas in too.
- **Creamy finish without the fat.** Blended **cottage cheese** off-heat gives makhani-style richness at ~84 kcal/100 g and +11 g protein/100 g — the lean stand-in for cream/butter (the new cottage-cheese lever). Silken tofu or light coconut milk are alternates; a knob of butter/cream if you want the classic.
- **Umami stack — and where it could go further.** Bhuna'd paste + garam + white miso carry it (tomato + miso glutamate). This base has **no GMP source**, so per the umami work it sits on the glutamate-only side — **a pinch (~2–3 g) of shiitake powder would multiply it** without adding obvious mushroom at that dose. Optional, but it's the highest-leverage umami add here.
- **Versatile base.** Stop after step 6 and you have a reusable smooth onion–tomato gravy — swap the chickpeas for air-fried tofu, paneer, butternut, or kofta.
