# Creamy Tomato, Coconut & Spinach Sauce v2 [02-07-26] ✴️

*A three-spice seasoning tweak on the [v1 sauce](creamy-tomato-coconut-spinach-sauce.md) that scored 8.5–9.1 on its first cook: the same tomato-paste-bhuna'd, coconut-milk-and-tofu base, plus 🍄 **shiitake powder** (off heat, umami multiplier — the same lever used in the sibling [Onion–Tomato Gravy](onion-tomato-gravy.md)), a pinch of 🟤 **cinnamon** (bloomed with the other spices), and a pinch of 🌰 **nutmeg** (off heat, the classic finish for a cream sauce). All three are stocked pantry items — cinnamon in particular is a run-down use-up priority. **Three variables at once, deliberately** — this isn't a controlled single-variable test, it's "let's see what all three together does"; if it lands well, a follow-up round can isolate which one mattered. **Not yet cooked.***

## Method

> **Blend the tofu first.** Blitz the 🍮 silken tofu smooth in the standalone blender before starting the pan.

1. **Bhuna the tomato paste.** Heat the 🥑 oil in the wok/pan, add the 🍅 tomato paste, and fry **~2 min**, stirring constantly so it cooks against the hot base and darkens.
2. **Bloom the aromatics, including the cinnamon.** Stir in the 🧄 garlic powder, 🌰 ground cumin, 🌿 ground coriander, and (**new**) a pinch of 🟤 ground cinnamon; bloom **~30 sec-1 min**, keeping it moving so the powders don't scorch. Go easy on the cinnamon — this is a savoury sauce, not a spiced one; it should read as warmth, not as "cinnamon."
3. **Loosen with coconut milk, simmer gently.** Pour in the 🥥 light coconut milk, stir to loosen the darkened paste into a sauce, and **simmer gently ~5 min**.
4. **Wilt the spinach.** Add the 🥬 fresh spinach and stir through **~2 min**, just until it collapses down into the sauce.
5. **Off heat: fold in the tofu, shiitake powder and nutmeg.** Pull the pan **off the heat** (or to the barest simmer) and stir in the blended 🍮 silken tofu, the 🧀 nutritional yeast, (**new**) a pinch of 🍄 shiitake powder, and (**new**) a pinch of 🌶️‍🟫 grated nutmeg — all four are finishing touches that don't want hard heat (shiitake powder loses its lift if boiled, nutmeg's aroma fades fast). 🧂 Salt to taste.
6. **Serve.** Spoon generously over the fanned hasselback potatoes, or — per Holger's idea — over air-fried cauliflower. 🌶️ Chilli flakes at the table for the adults.

## Design notes

- **Three simultaneous seasoning changes, on purpose — not a controlled test.** The project's iteration philosophy (`../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md`) prefers single-variable changes for clean attribution, but Holger explicitly asked to try all three together. If v2 scores well, a follow-up round dropping one variable at a time (starting with cinnamon, the riskiest for Lara) would isolate which one is carrying it.
- **Shiitake powder is the safest of the three** — it's this project's established umami lever for exactly this sauce family (see the [Onion–Tomato Gravy](onion-tomato-gravy.md)'s design notes: the base is glutamate-only, and shiitake powder multiplies it without reading as mushroom). No known family sensitivity to it.
- **Cinnamon is the one to watch for Lara.** She's already got one untested variable in this sauce (cumin, swapped in on the actual cook — see `../../docs/RATINGS.md` remark 51) plus a separately-flagged, unconfirmed wariness of warm aromatics (cinnamon/cardamom/galangal). Kept to 1 g deliberately small so it reads as background warmth, not a cinnamon note — but if v2 underperforms with her specifically, this is the first suspect.
- **Nutmeg is classic technique, low risk.** A pinch of nutmeg at the end is the standard finish for cream-based sauces (béchamel, makhani-style gravies) — subtle, not on anyone's flagged list, added off heat because its aroma is delicate and fades (or turns bitter) under prolonged heat.
- **Also floated: serving this on air-fried cauliflower instead of (or alongside) the potatoes** — fits the established pattern where Jannes tolerates cauliflower when it's coated in something bold (obs 19). Not built into this file since it's a serving-context change, not a sauce change; the cornflour-thickening idea for that application is still a separate, later experiment.
- **Nutritionally a rounding error vs. v1** — 5 g of spices across a 1.16 kg batch barely moves the macros. The interesting nutrition note is qualitative: shiitake is a natural (if small) vitamin D source, so this is the first version of the sauce with a nonzero Vitamin D figure.
