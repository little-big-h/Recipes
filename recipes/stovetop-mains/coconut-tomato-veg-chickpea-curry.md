# Creamy Coconut–Tomato Veg & Chickpea Curry

*A mild, family creamy curry: a deep **browned-onion base** (microwave-and-blend shortcut, then bhuna'd), spiced with the stocked medium curry powder, with **tomato purée + light coconut milk + blended silken tofu** for a fat-light cream. Air-fried cauliflower folded in for caramelised texture, chickpeas for protein, potatoes for carbs. Built deliberately to **dodge the Ninja scorch trap** — the sweet onion-tomato base never goes under pressure (potatoes are microwave par-cooked and finished in the sauce). Mild in the pot; chilli + lime at the table for the adults. Family of five.*

## Method

> **Three things de-risk this cook:** the potatoes are par-cooked *out* of the pot (no scorch), the cauliflower is air-fried *ahead* (caramelised, holds shape), and the coconut + tofu go in *off the heat* (no split).

> **Make ahead — the chickpeas.** Pressure-cook ~250 g dried 🫘 chickpeas in plain water (~800 ml–1 L; **no tomato** — acid stalls bean softening) in the Ninja, natural release: **unsoaked 30–40 min, or soaked 12–14 min** (Nussinow). Drain (reserve a little liquid). Yields ~660 g cooked. *(A tin of pre-cooked chickpeas, ~660 g drained, skips this.)*

1. **Air-fry the cauliflower ahead.** Toss the 🌼 cauliflower in a little oil + salt (a pinch of the curry powder, optional). **200 °C, ~18–20 min**, shaking once, until browned at the edges. Single layer — two batches beats crowding. Set aside; it folds in at the very end.
2. **Par-cook the potatoes.** Cube the 🥔 potatoes into a covered microwave bowl with a splash of water; **high ~6–8 min, stir once**, to *firm-tender* (not fully soft — they finish in the sauce). Drain.
3. **Onion base (the shortcut).** Microwave the 🧅 onion to soften, then **blend to a purée**. Bhuna the purée in the 🥑 oil on the Ninja sauté — fry → it catches → splash water → scrape the fond up → repeat — until **deep gold** (it's *prep*, not the browning; the frying is what builds the umami). See `../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md` → Bhuna.
4. **Spices.** Add 🧄 garlic + 🫚 ginger, fry off the raw smell; add the 🍛 curry powder, bloom ~30 sec; add the 🍅 tomato purée (concentrate — *not* a passata-volume) and **bhuna ~1–2 min** until darkened, splashing water as it catches.
5. **Sauce + potatoes — no pressure.** Add the par-cooked 🥔 potatoes + 💧 water to a loose sauce; **simmer on sauté 5–10 min** (stir, no pressure → no scorch from the sweet base) to finish the potatoes and infuse them. Stir the 🫘 chickpeas in to warm.
6. **Greens.** Add the 🥦 broccoli (air-fried with the cauliflower, or simmered 3–4 min here — your call).
7. **Cream, off the heat.** Stir in the 🥥 light coconut milk and the **blended** 🍮 silken tofu; **don't boil after** (both split). 🧂 Salt to taste.
8. **Finish.** Fold in the air-fried 🌼 cauliflower (and 🥦 broccoli, if air-fried) last so it keeps its edges. 🌿 Coriander, a squeeze of 🍋 lemon. 🌶️ Chilli + lime at the table for the adults.

## Design notes

- **No pressure-cook for the potatoes — by design.** The bhuna'd onion + concentrated tomato base is sweet and sticky; under pressure it scorches against the Ninja's hot base *even with added water* (the sugars settle and catch). So the potatoes are microwave par-cooked and finished in the sauce on sauté, where you can stir. Pressure was only ever a time-saver here, and the air-fryer's running in parallel anyway.
- **Onion base via the microwave-and-blend shortcut** — softened + blended onion bhuna'd to deep gold is the mild curry's umami engine (browned onion = sweet savoury depth, no heat). The shortcut is *prep*; the bhuna still does the browning. See `../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md`.
- **Cauliflower air-fried, folded late** — caramelised florets that hold shape, the way Jannes tolerates cauliflower (sauced, not boiled to mush).
- **Cream = light coconut milk + blended silken tofu, off heat.** The tofu adds body and protein near fat-free; both split if boiled, so they go in at the end. **Blend the tofu smooth** — Lara's fine with silken tofu fully blended, not in recognisable lumps.
- **Tomato purée ≠ passata.** Concentrate, so ~60 g (a few tbsp) bhuna'd, with the liquid built from coconut milk + water — not a passata-sized pour.
- **Beans off the acid.** Dried chickpeas are pressure-cooked separately in plain water (make-ahead above), then stirred in — never cooked in the tomato base, which stalls bean softening.
- **Mild for the family**, chilli + lime at the table for the adults — the curry powder is chilli-free, so the pot stays gentle.
