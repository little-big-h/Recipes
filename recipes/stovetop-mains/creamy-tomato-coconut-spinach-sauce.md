# Creamy Tomato, Coconut & Spinach Sauce [02-07-26] ✴️

*A quick **topping sauce**: 🍅 tomato paste bhuna'd for depth, loosened with 🥥 light coconut milk, 🥬 fresh spinach wilted in for iron and colour, then blended 🍮 silken tofu stirred through off the heat for body and protein. A coconut-creamy cousin of the [Smooth Onion–Tomato Gravy](onion-tomato-gravy.md) — built smaller and faster (~11 min active), designed to run **in parallel** with the [Hasselback Jacket Potatoes](../oven-mains/hasselback-jacket-potatoes.md) pipeline so it costs no extra time on the clock. Mild, smooth (Lara-friendly — tofu fully blended). **As actually cooked (2026-07-02)** the batch ran bigger — a full can of coconut milk, doubled tofu, more tomato paste and garlic powder, cumin + coriander in for the planned ginger — landing at ~1 kg rather than the planned ~600 g.*

## Method

> **Blend the tofu first.** Blitz the 🍮 silken tofu smooth in the standalone blender before starting the pan — Lara dislikes recognisable silken tofu in a dish, and it's one less thing to do while the pan is active.

1. **Bhuna the tomato paste.** Heat the 🥑 oil in the wok/pan, add the 🍅 tomato paste, and fry **~2 min**, stirring constantly so it cooks against the hot base and darkens — the whole flavour base, same principle as the onion-tomato gravy's bhuna step.
2. **Bloom the aromatics.** Stir in the 🧄 garlic powder and 🌰 ground cumin + 🌿 ground coriander (as cooked 2026-07-02 — the original design used 🫚 ground ginger instead; either works); bloom **~30 sec-1 min**, keeping it moving so the powders don't scorch.
3. **Loosen with coconut milk, simmer gently.** Pour in the 🥥 light coconut milk, stir to loosen the darkened paste into a sauce, and **simmer gently ~5 min** — a light bubble, not a hard boil; coconut milk tolerates gentle heat fine (it's the tofu, not the coconut milk, that's fragile).
4. **Wilt the spinach.** Add the 🥬 fresh spinach and stir through **~2 min**, just until it collapses down into the sauce.
5. **Off heat: fold in the tofu and nutritional yeast.** Pull the pan **off the heat** (or to the barest simmer) and stir in the blended 🍮 silken tofu — boiling it now would split the emulsion (per `../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md`). If it goes grainy, a splash more coconut milk and off the heat rescues it. Stir in the 🧀 nutritional yeast for extra savoury depth (as cooked 2026-07-02 — not in the original plan). 🧂 Salt to taste.
6. **Serve.** Spoon generously over the fanned hasselback potatoes. 🌶️ Chilli flakes at the table for the adults — the pot stays mild.

## Design notes

- **Split heat-handling is the whole technique here.** Three ingredients, three different heat rules: 🍅 tomato paste wants real heat (bhuna to build flavour), 🥥 coconut milk tolerates a gentle simmer, and 🍮 silken tofu is off-heat-or-barest-simmer only (boiling splits it — `../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md`). Sequencing hot-to-gentle-to-off is what keeps the sauce smooth.
- **Structural cousin of the [Smooth Onion–Tomato Gravy](onion-tomato-gravy.md), not a variant of it.** Same bhuna-the-paste instinct, but no onion base, no whole tomatoes to reduce, and coconut milk + tofu stand in for the blended-whole-tomato body — a genuinely different, faster route to a creamy tomato sauce. Filed as its own recipe rather than a `-v2` for that reason.
- **Built to run in parallel, not in series.** At ~11 min active time it comfortably fits inside the hasselback potatoes' ~23 min pipeline — there's no point treating this as "another dish to cook afterwards." Start it once the potatoes are cut and the first microwave pair is under way.
- **Blend-smooth tofu = Lara-friendly**, same rule as every other silken-tofu dish in the project. Blending it *before* the pan is active means one less thing to juggle mid-cook.
- **Fresh spinach, not frozen** — it's not in `docs/PANTRY.md`'s always-in-stock list, so confirm what's actually on hand before shopping; frozen spinach (drained well) would substitute directly if that's what's in the freezer.
- **Mild in the pot.** No chilli, no smoked paprika — chilli flakes go at the table per Holger's standard build-your-own pattern, keeping the base clear for Lara and Jannes.
- **Pantry run-down.** Every ingredient (tomato paste, coconut milk, silken tofu, garlic powder, cumin, coriander) is a stocked staple — nothing bought for this one.
- **Cumin + coriander instead of ginger (actual cook).** A straight swap of the ground-aromatic pairing — same bloom-in-oil step, same role. Not yet known whether it reads as more "curry-adjacent" than the planned ginger version; worth comparing on a repeat cook.
- **Nutritional yeast folded in with the tofu (actual cook).** 15 g at step 5, off heat alongside the tofu — adds savoury depth and protein. Not in the original four-ingredient design; worth keeping if it lands well, since it's a stocked staple either way.
- **Batch size scaled up substantially, not uniformly.** Tomato paste, garlic powder, tofu and spinach all roughly doubled; coconut milk went to a full can (1.6x); nutritional yeast is new. Too many simultaneous changes to isolate which one drove any taste shift — but there's a large amount of leftover sauce for more than four potatoes' worth of topping.
- **Spinach weight (300 g) was initially deduced from the yield, then confirmed directly** — the mass-balance estimate (~300 g, closing the gap between 856 g of other ingredients and the 1159 g yield to within 3 g) matched the real figure almost exactly. A useful sanity-check technique for future cooks with an unreported ingredient, not just a one-off guess.
