# Potato & Spinach Curry with Air-Fried Tofu

*A weeknight coconut curry: 🥔 potato **air-fried separately** (caramelised edges instead of boiled-soft), folded into an 🧅 onion bhuna finished with 🍛 mild curry powder, a bhuna'd 🍅 tomato purée and 🥥 light coconut milk, with 🥬 spinach wilted in at the end. 🧊 Tofu air-fries as its own side, timed to land hot at the same moment. Mild and family-friendly — no chilli in the pot. Family of five. *(Originally designed with cauliflower too — pulled out mid-cook into its own separate dish; see Design notes.)*

## Method

1. **Toss and start the potato.** Cube the 🥔 potato, toss with ~5 g 🥑 oil. Air-fry **200C, ~20 min**, shaking once, until almost done (not fully soft — it finishes in the sauce).
2. **Jumpstart the onion.** While the potato fries, microwave the 🧅 onion (chopped) ~2-3 min to pre-soften — the standard scorch-avoidance move before it hits the wok.
3. **Bhuna the onion.** Transfer to the wok with a little 🥑 oil; cook until deep golden, ~5-6 min.
4. **Bloom the curry powder.** Add the 🍛 mild curry powder late, once the onion is properly bhuna'd — stir ~30-60 sec in the residual oil to wake up the spices before any liquid goes in.
5. **Bhuna the tomato purée.** Add the 🍅 tomato purée and cook it down ~1 min until it visibly darkens — the umami anchor, same move as the lobia masala's tomato paste. Judge it by colour, not the clock.
6. **Add the coconut milk.** Pour in the 🥥 light coconut milk to deglaze, stir to combine, bring to a gentle simmer — not a rolling boil, or the light (lower-fat) coconut milk can split.
7. **Transfer the potato.** Once the air-fried 🥔 potato is almost done, fold it into the sauce to finish cooking through and pick up flavour.
8. **Tofu, separately.** Into the now-free air fryer: cube the 🧊 tofu, toss with ~5 g 🥑 oil, air-fry **200C, ~15 min**, shaking once, until firm and golden.
9. **Fold in the spinach.** Add the raw 🥬 spinach to the wok; wilt through, ~2-3 min, right at the end so it doesn't overcook.
10. **Serve** — curry with the air-fried tofu on the side. Taste and salt at the table — the pot itself is low-salt by design.

## Design notes

- **Cauliflower split off into its own separate dish, mid-cook (2026-07-14).** This recipe was originally designed as a potato/cauliflower/spinach curry; Holger pulled the cauliflower out partway through cooking to make it its own dish instead, and increased the spinach in this one to compensate. The 500 g planned / 967.5 g actual cauliflower figures that were briefly logged here belong to that other dish, not this one — removed from this file's Ingredients table and nutrition accordingly. File renamed from `potato-cauliflower-spinach-curry-tofu.md` to `potato-spinach-curry-tofu.md` to match.
- **Potato air-fried separately, not boiled in the sauce.** Same move as the lobia masala's butternut and the tikka curry's cauliflower — caramelised edges from dry heat, folded in late rather than dissolved into the sauce. Trade-off worth naming: air-frying browns and slightly seals the surface, so it absorbs less of the curry's flavour than a raw potato simmered straight in the sauce would — the choice here favours the caramelised edges and firmer bite over maximum flavour penetration.
- **Onion bhuna is the umami anchor**, same architecture as the coconut-tomato veg & chickpea curry and the lobia masala — microwave-jumpstart to avoid scorching, then a real bhuna in the wok before any liquid goes in.
- **Curry powder added late, after the onion is properly browned** — blooming it in residual oil rather than at the start avoids it scorching before the onion's own sugars have developed.
- **Tomato purée bhuna'd before the coconut milk goes in** — the same umami-anchor move as the lobia masala's tomato paste ("a tomato base goes flat without one"). Cooked down until it visibly darkens, not on a timer.
- **No chilli in the pot** — mild by design, family-friendly. Heat can go at the table if wanted.
- **Tofu genuinely separate, not folded into the sauce** — a plain air-fried side rather than simmered/blended in, so it stays firm and distinct rather than picking up the curry's moisture.
- **Confirmed: the ML750 has enough basket capacity for the potato to air-fry as one batch**, not the cautious two-batch default used elsewhere in the project (hasselback potatoes, the soybean-corn soup) where capacity was never actually confirmed — resolves that open assumption, at least for a load this size.
- **Quantities still mid-reconciliation.** Potato (1548 g) and onion (411.5 g) actuals are logged; curry powder, tomato purée and coconut milk are assumed as originally specified (30 g / 50 g / 400 ml) pending confirmation; spinach's actual amount and the dish's cooked yield are still to come. The Nutrition section and `.foodnoms` link above are the **planned** baseline (cauliflower removed, oil reduced accordingly) — will be rebuilt against full actuals once everything's in.
