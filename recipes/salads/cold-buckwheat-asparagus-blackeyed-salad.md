# Cold Buckwheat, Asparagus & Black-Eyed Bean Salad — Peanut–Sweet-Chilli (Gado-Gado-Style)

*A hot-day, make-ahead, **build-your-own** cold salad: a toasted-🌾 buckwheat base under chilled 🫛 asparagus, firm black-eyed 🫘 beans, halved hard-boiled 🥚 eggs, raw 🫑 peppers and charred 🌽 corn, dressed with a low-fat **peanut-flour + sweet-chilli-coconut-aminos + pomegranate-molasses** dressing. Mild and creamy for the kids (the dressing cut with **🥛 yoghurt**), with chilli + 🍋 lime + extra 🍇 pom **at the table** for the adults. Everything cooks low-effort at lunchtime and chills all afternoon. Family of five.*

## Method

> **Two firmness rules for a cold salad:** the 🫘 beans and the 🌾 buckwheat both turn to mush if over-softened, so cook them *just-tender* and **drain + spread to cool fast** — chilling halts the carryover that softens them.

**Lunchtime (low-effort, then chill):**

1. **🫘 Beans.** Pressure-cook 300 g dried black-eyed 🫘 beans in plain water (~900 ml–1 L; **no tomato** — *acid* stalls 🫘 beans, but salt does not). **High pressure 4 min, natural release** (down ~2 min from the usual 5–6, since last time was mushy; never quick-release). The instant pressure's down, **drain and spread on a tray to cool** — this is the real anti-mush move. *Optional flavour upgrade: cook them in a **light veg/herb stock** (½–1 cube) instead of plain water — it seasons the 🫘 beans from the inside (salt even helps them cook evenly, skins intact). Use a **plain, not tomato-based** stock, go light (the dressing already brings salt), and keep it 4 min. You drain off the liquid, so nutritionally it's negligible — only a little extra sodium carries into the 🫘 beans.*
2. **🥚 Eggs.** Hard-boil 6 🥚 eggs (~10 min), straight into iced water, peel. Chill.
3. **🌾 Kasha.** It's **already toasted**, so skip the dry-pan step: simmer ~1:2 with water ~10 min to *just tender*, drain, spread to cool. Toss the cooled grain with a splash of the 🫗 aminos + 🍋 lime so the base is seasoned.
4. Everything into the fridge for the afternoon.

**Pre-dinner (~15 min):**

5. **🫛 Asparagus.** Blanch 2 min, then **straight into cold water** to keep it crisp and green (or griddle-then-chill for a smoky edge). Cut into salad lengths.
6. **🌽 Corn.** Blister the drained 🌽 corn in a dry hot pan until spotty-brown (charring beats plain), then cool.
7. **🫑 Peppers.** Dice raw — crunchy, sweet, and Lara-safe (she dislikes *cooked* 🫑 peppers; raw is fine).
8. **🥜 Dressing.** Whisk 🥜 peanut flour + 🫗 sweet-chilli aminos + a small drizzle of 🍇 pom molasses + grated 🧄 garlic/ginger, loosened with water to a pourable cream (it thickens as it sits). Taste; a pinch of nooch/shiitake if it reads flat.
9. **Split for the family.** Cut about half the dressing with the 🥛 yoghurt → the **mild, creamy kids' version** (dairy casein genuinely tames the chilli). Keep the rest neat.
10. **Build-your-own.** 🌾 Buckwheat base → 🫛 asparagus, 🫘 beans, halved 🥚 eggs, raw 🫑 peppers, charred 🌽 corn → dressing of choice → 🌰 toasted peanuts + 🌿 herbs. **At the table:** 🍋 lime, extra chilli, extra 🍇 pom molasses for the adults.

## Design notes

- **Make-ahead by design.** The three low-effort cooks (🫘 beans, 🥚 eggs, 🌾 kasha) all *want* to cool anyway, so they go in at lunchtime and chill for hours; only the quick fresh bits (🫛 asparagus, 🌽 corn, 🫑 peppers, dressing) happen at dinner. Hot-day-friendly.
- **Firmer 🫘 beans = lower time + fast cooling.** 4 min HP (vs the usual 5–6) and an immediate drain-and-spread — natural-release carryover was what mushed them last time, and chilling stops it.
- **No kecap → sweet-chilli aminos + pom molasses.** The 🫗 Biona Sweet Chilli coconut aminos is dark, sweet *and* mildly chilli'd, so it stands in for kecap's sweet-salty depth on its own; the 🍇 pomegranate molasses adds the fruity-tart molasses note (muhammara logic — pom + peanut + pepper). 🥜 Defatted peanut flour keeps the whole dressing oil-free.
- **Threads every family preference.** Jannes controls heat (build-your-own + 🥛 yoghurt-cut base); **Lara gets raw 🫑 peppers** (her cooked-pepper dislike doesn't apply) and the acid held to the table; the kids get 🌽 corn and the creamy mild dressing. 🥛 Yoghurt's casein is a real capsaicin tamer, not just dilution.
- **Cottage-cheese swap (future).** Per `docs/IDEAS.md`, blended cottage cheese could replace the 🥛 yoghurt in the kids' dressing for more protein — test next time.
