# Peanut–Kecap Asparagus & Sprout Bowl (Gado-Gado-Style)

*A quick solo bowl Holger threw together and rated **very good**, now reconciled to his actual FoodNoms log (2026-06-10): steamed asparagus and bean sprouts tossed in a loose **peanut-flour + kecap manis** sauce. Essentially a deconstructed **gado-gado** — peanut sauce over veg + sprouts. High-protein, low-fat, bright. Single serving. Values from the logged labels (see notes). **The logged instance left out the capers** that made the original sing — see the callout below; an acid is not optional here.*

> **⚪ Add the capers (or another acid).** The original version had **~15 g capers** — they supply the sharp brine-acid that cuts the sweet-rich peanut-kecap base (the role tamarind/lime plays in real gado-gado). This logged lunch omitted them and leaned on lime instead. **Don't skip the acid**: without it the peanut + kecap reads flat and cloying. ---

## Method

1. **Sauce.** Whisk the 🥜 peanut flour with the 🍯 kecap manis and a splash of warm water into a loose, pourable sauce — sweet-salty-nutty.
2. **Asparagus.** Steam the 🥬 asparagus 2–3 min, kept crisp.
3. **Toss** the asparagus and 🌱 sprouts (and 🫒 capers, if using) through the sauce.
4. **Finish** with 🍋 lime — or the caper brine. **The acid is the point.**

## Design notes — why it works

- **It's a deconstructed gado-gado.** Peanut sauce over blanched veg *and* bean sprouts is the canonical structure.
- **Peanut + kecap manis = a sweet-salt-umami block that's cloying alone.** It needs a sharp sour note — tamarind in the bumbu, lime over gado-gado. **Capers** do that job brilliantly (brine + acid + mustardy pungency): a non-Indonesian ingredient filling the cuisine's structural need. Lime is the lighter, more authentic alternative.
- **The peanut flour is only lightly defatted** (~14 % fat), so it's not as fat-free as a fully-defatted flour — but at 10 g it still keeps the plate lean while carrying the protein.
- **Sprouts logged as generic USDA "Sprouts" (`2709764`); mung bean sprouts (`169957`) are the better match** — near-identical values, and canonical for gado-gado.
- **Next-time acids** (all stocked): capers, lime, tamarind, rice vinegar — each tilts it differently.
