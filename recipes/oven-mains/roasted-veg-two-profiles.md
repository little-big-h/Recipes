# Mediterranean & Indian Roasted Vegetable Tray

*Same vegetables, two flavour profiles — split the tray before seasoning. Butternut squash, cauliflower, peppers, onions and cherry tomatoes. Served with kasha (0 min pressure cook — heat-up only). Family of five.*

> **Migrated from legacy PDF source** (`roasted-veg-two-profiles.pdf`). No family ratings on record.

> **Design pattern:** the split-tray approach handles mixed family preferences in a single oven session. Both trays use the same base vegetables but get separate seasoning treatments. The Indian tray gets a finishing miso-tamarind treatment after roasting; the Mediterranean tray just gets capers.

> ⚠️ **Both trays carry Lara concerns:**
> - **Cooked peppers** in both trays
> - **Smoked paprika** in both trays (Med: 2 tsp ≈ 4 g; Indian: 2 g)
> - **Med tray** adds balsamic + capers (acid)
> - **Indian tray** adds tamarind (sour) at end
>
> Plus **cauliflower** is in both trays (Jannes concern).
>
> Not the most family-friendly recipe in the project. Build-your-own from the two trays gives some flexibility, but the structural conflicts are real.

## Method

> **Split-tray:** divide all vegetables into two roughly equal batches before seasoning. Each tray gets its own oil + spices. Both trays roast simultaneously on different oven shelves.

1. (0–15 min) Preheat oven to 200°C. Chop 🎃 squash and 🧅 onions into large chunks, 🥦 cauliflower into florets, 🫑 peppers into large pieces.

2. (15–20 min) **Split** vegetables into two batches. Toss each with 🫒 olive oil, ⚫ salt and pepper, then season separately:

   - **Mediterranean batch:** 🌿 rosemary (tucked in), 🪴 oregano, 🟥 smoked paprika, 🧄 garlic powder, 🍷 balsamic
   - **Indian batch:** 🟠 mild curry, 🟫 dried ginger, 🌶️ chili flakes, 🧄 garlic powder, 🌰 smoked paprika, 🍄 shiitake powder

3. (20–35 min) Roast 🎃 squash and 🧅 onions in both trays at 200°C for 15 minutes.

4. (15–19 min, in parallel) Toast 🌾 kasha dry in Ninja on sauté mode 3–4 min, add 600 ml water, seal — high pressure, **0 min** pressure cook, then delayed release. Heat-up (18–21 min) is sufficient.

5. (35–45 min) Add 🥦 cauliflower and 🫑 peppers to **both trays**. Roast a further 10 minutes.

6. (45–55 min) Add halved 🍅 cherry tomatoes to both trays. Roast final 10 minutes until collapsed and caramelised.

7. (~55 min) Remove from oven. Finish each tray separately:

   - **Mediterranean:** scatter 🟢 capers over. Remove 🌿 rosemary sprigs.
   - **Indian:** dissolve 🫙 white miso in a hot corner of the tray, stir in 🥥 tamarind, mix through.

8. Serve over 🌾 kasha — let people choose their tray (or take from both).
