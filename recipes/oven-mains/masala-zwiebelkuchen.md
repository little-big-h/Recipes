# Masala-Zwiebelkuchen [07-11-26] ✴️

*A German/South-Asian savoury bake for the Christmas event at the German school. Sheet-pan Zwiebelkuchen with two substitutions doing the work: the **Kümmel becomes Kreuzkümmel** — caraway out, cumin in, the two words a prefix apart in German — and the Speck becomes browned mushroom — 🍄 king oyster and 🍄‍🟫 shiitake. The base is **100% atta**, which is the only structural fusion element in the dish; everything else South Asian here is a seasoning. No chilli, no white flour, and 🥬 nothing that needs cutlery. Cuts into roughly 24 squares eaten by hand.*

> ⚠ **COOKED 07-11-2026, and the quantities above are what actually went in**, not the plan. Where they differ from the design the reason is written beside them. Still outstanding: the sweated-onion weight, the two mushrooms out of the oven, and the baked tray — see **Weigh-ins** under Design notes. Full reasoning, including everything considered and rejected, is in [`design/MASALA-ZWIEBELKUCHEN.md`](../../design/MASALA-ZWIEBELKUCHEN.md).

## Method

### 1. Dough — start first, it gates everything

1. **Autolyse.** Mix the 🫓 atta and ~230 ml warm 💧 alone. Rest **30 minutes**. No yeast, no salt, no oil yet.
   > The highest-value step in this dough and it costs only clock time. The bran hydrates and softens so its edges cut less gluten, and gluten develops without kneading.
2. Add 🫧 yeast, 🧂 4 g salt, 💪 16 g vital wheat gluten and 🥑 32 g oil. Knead to a smooth, slightly tacky dough. **Judge the water by feel** — atta's chakki-damaged starch is unusually thirsty and 230 ml is a starting point, not a target.
3. Rise until roughly doubled, about **80 minutes**.

### 2. Onions — small wok

4. **Tadka:** bloom 🌰 4 g cumin, 🌼 mustard and 🌿 fennel in 🥑 15 g oil until the seeds pop. Then 🫚 ginger, then 🌻 turmeric.
5. 🧅 Onions in on top of that, with 🧀 nooch, 🍄‍🟫 shiitake powder and 🧂 1 g salt. **Sweat soft with no colour.**
   > ⚠ **Pale, not caramelised.** Zwiebelkuchen onion is juicy and pale. Browning collapses 1250 g to a fraction and buries the spices under sweetness. This is the opposite of the 26-10 butter masala, where caramelising was right.
6. 🥭 **Amchur in at the very end.** It is a dried fruit powder and long dry heat dulls it and pushes it bitter.

### 3. Speck layer — oven

7. 🍄 King oyster and 🍄‍🟫 shiitake caps in 8 mm dice, **browned hard and dry.** Oil (🥑 10 g) only once the surfaces have gone matte. The two can share a tray — both are mushroom and shed water similarly — but **shiitake caps are thinner and colour first, so pull them when they are done** rather than waiting for the king oyster.
   > ⚠ **Shiitake caps only.** The stems stay woody through a bake. Bag them for the freezer, where they are the dashi ingredient.
   > ⚠⚠ **This layer was 200 g tau kwa + 250 g king oyster until 07-11-2026.** Only silken tofu was in the house on the day, and silken cannot do this job at all — at roughly 90% water and set with GDL, 8 mm cubes collapse the moment they meet a hot pan. **Mushroom took the whole layer rather than tofu taking a larger share**, because the tofu was there for discrete pieces and the mushroom for chew, and a layer that is mostly pale wet cubes is the exact failure the two-component substitution existed to avoid.
   > Bare preheated metal, **not paper and not a rack** — see `../../docs/TECHNIQUES.md`.

### 4. Base

8. Press the dough thin into the tray with a **2 cm rim**. **The tray is 40 × 30 cm**, measured 2026-11-07.
9. **Pre-bake 12 minutes at 220 °C, convection with fan.** Not traditional, and necessary: a thin base under a wet topping goes soggy underneath, and a square that cannot be picked up by hand is useless at a buffet. 12 rather than 10 because wholemeal is more porous and lets topping moisture through faster.

### 5. Custard and assembly

10. **Slake** 🥔 11 g potato starch into a little cold 🥛 soya milk, then whisk in the rest of the milk and the 🥚 eggs. Dropped in dry it lumps.
    > ⚠ **11 g, not the 15 g the plan specified for corn flour.** Potato starch thickens roughly **1.3× as hard per gram**. It was the only starch in the house on 07-11-2026, and it satisfies the zero-white-flour rule the same way corn flour does — potato, not wheat.
    > ⚠ Potato starch's weakness is that it peaks fast and then **thins under prolonged heat**, where corn starch holds. That barely matters here: the three 🥚 are the primary setting agent and the starch is a supporting binder. In a stovetop sauce held at a simmer it would matter a great deal.
    > ⚠ Costed in the `.foodnoms` against the SR Legacy **cornstarch** record. Both are over 99% pure starch so the swap is nutritionally immaterial at 11 g, and the branded potato starch records are sparse and look label-rounded.
    > ⚠ **No acid in the custard.** Soy protein flocculates near pH 4.5. The dish's acid is the 🥭 amchur in the onions, deliberately.
11. Onions over the base, then the browned 🍄 and 🍄‍🟫, then pour the custard over.
12. 🌰 **2 g whole cumin scattered on top, and nothing else.** This is the whole joke and it has to be seen.

### 6. Bake

13. **200 °C convection with fan, 35–40 minutes**, until the custard is set and the surface is coloured. Cool before cutting.

## Design notes

- **The fusion is two substitutions, not a pile of spices.** Kümmel → Kreuzkümmel on top, and Speck → browned tau kwa plus king oyster. The atta base is the third and the only structural one.
- **Why the Speck layer is all mushroom.** Speck gives discrete chewy pieces, fat, salt and smoke. Tau kwa was in the design for the pieces and the protein, and king oyster for the chew — but only silken tofu was available on the day and silken cannot be diced or browned. Mushroom took the whole layer rather than tofu taking a larger share, because pale wet cubes in a custard are the standard failure of this substitution. Smoke is dropped rather than faked. ⚠ **The cost is about 26 g of protein across the tray**, which is why the per-square protein drops from 5.8 g to 4.9 g.
- **1316 g of onion is not a typo.** The onion volume is the body of the dish, and a thin onion layer is the most common way this bake fails.
- **Zero white flour is a rule.** If the base comes out crumbly the ladder is: more gluten (15 → 25 g), then less oil (fat shortens dough, so cutting it helps), then a thicker base, then an egg in the dough. Sifting out the bran is not on the ladder — that is making white flour by hand.
- **Considered and rejected:** chestnuts, sumac, lemongrass and makrut lime leaves, and ordinary wholemeal in place of atta. Each with reasoning in the design doc, so none of them gets re-proposed.
