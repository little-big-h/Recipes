# Mediterranean Roasted Vegetable Tray with Kasha

*Rosemary-roasted butternut squash, cauliflower, peppers, onions and cherry tomatoes with balsamic and capers. Served with kasha (0 min pressure cook). Family of five.*

> **Migrated from legacy PDF source** (`roasted-veg-mediterranean.pdf`). No family ratings on record.

> ⚠️ **Multiple Lara concerns** — cooked peppers, smoked paprika, and balsamic + capers (acid). Plus a Jannes concern (cauliflower).
>
> **Lara-friendlier substitutions:** drop the bell peppers (or quarter and put on Holger's side of the tray), halve the smoked paprika and double the oregano, move the balsamic to a finishing drizzle (so only some of the dish carries it).

> **Two-profile variant** lives in [roasted-veg-two-profiles.md](./roasted-veg-two-profiles.md) — same vegetables split across two trays with Mediterranean on one and Indian seasoning on the other. Useful for mixed family preferences.

## Method

> **Staged roasting:** robust vegetables (squash, onions) go in first. Delicate ones (cauliflower, peppers) at minute 35. Tomatoes last at minute 45 — they collapse and add jamminess in 10 minutes. Total roast time about 35 minutes for the long-cookers, 20 minutes for the medium ones, 10 minutes for tomatoes.

1. (0–15 min) Preheat oven to 200°C. Chop 🎃 squash and 🧅 onions into large chunks, 🥦 cauliflower into florets, 🫑 peppers into large pieces.

2. (15–20 min) Toss all vegetables with 🫒 olive oil, 🧂 salt, pepper, 🌶️ smoked paprika, 🧄 garlic powder and 🍷 balsamic vinegar. Tuck 🌿 rosemary sprigs throughout. Sprinkle 🪴 dried oregano over.

3. (20–35 min) Roast 🎃 squash and 🧅 onions at 200°C for 15 minutes.

4. (15–19 min, in parallel) Toast 🌾 kasha dry in Ninja on sauté mode 3–4 min, add 600 ml water, seal — high pressure, **0 min** pressure cook, then delayed release. Heat-up (18–21 min) is sufficient to cook kasha through; the pressure setting at 0 minutes just engages the seal-and-release cycle.

5. (35–45 min) Add 🥦 cauliflower and 🫑 peppers to the tray. Roast a further 10 minutes.

6. (45–55 min) Add halved 🍅 cherry tomatoes. Roast final 10 minutes until collapsed and lightly caramelised.

7. (~55 min) Remove from oven. Scatter 🫛 capers over. Remove 🌿 rosemary sprigs before serving.

8. Serve over 🌾 kasha.
