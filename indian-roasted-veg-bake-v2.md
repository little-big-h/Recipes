# Indian Roasted Vegetable Bake (v2)

*Broccoli and carrots in a pure creamed corn, silken tofu and curry sauce. Carrots, onions, garlic and chickpeas air-fried in Ninja; broccoli mixed into sauce and poured over. Cherry tomatoes added last. Served with cottage cheese. Family of five.*

> **Migrated from legacy PDF source** (`indian-roasted-veg-bake-v2.pdf`). No family ratings on record.

> **Reformulation insight — this is a Lara/Jannes-friendlier redesign of v1.** Worth studying as a worked example of recipe iteration responding to family preferences:
>
> | | v1 | v2 |
> |:-|:-|:-|
> | **Cauliflower** (Jannes dislikes) | 1 head | removed |
> | **Bell peppers** (Lara: cooked peppers) | 2 | removed |
> | **Replacement vegetables** | — | carrots 400g + chickpeas 1 tin |
> | **Sauce base** | Tinned tomatoes + curry | Creamed corn + silken tofu + curry (creamy, not tomato-acidic) |
> | **Protein** | none specifically added | Chickpeas (22g) + silken tofu (300g blended) + cottage cheese side |
> | **Technique** | Oven-only, two waves | Ninja air-fry + oven combo |
> | **Acid** | None in pot | Lemon at end (mild, finishing) |
>
> The v2 reformulation removes both flagged-dislike ingredients while substantially increasing protein density and creating a creamier, kid-friendlier sauce.

> **Cottage cheese note:** served cold on the side. Do not stir into the hot bake — it splits and loses its texture. This is a build-your-own component, not a sauce ingredient.

## Method

> **Two parallel prep streams + air fry + oven finish.** Carrots, onions, garlic and chickpeas get air-fried for caramelisation; broccoli goes in raw, mixed into the sauce. Everything combines in the oven for the final 22 minutes.

1. (0–10 min) Preheat oven to 200°C. Chop 🥕 carrots into thick slices, 🧅 onions into wedges, 🧄 garlic cloves left whole. Drain and pat 🫘 chickpeas dry. In parallel, chop 🥦 broccoli into florets (0–15 min).

2. (~5–20 min, in parallel) Dissolve 🧊 stock cube in 200 ml hot water. Bloom 🟡 mild curry powder and 🟠 turmeric with 1 tsp 🫒 oil in a microwave-safe bowl, 30–60 seconds. Blend with 🌽 creamed corn, ⚪ silken tofu and stock until completely smooth and pourable. Mix 🥦 broccoli florets into the sauce.

3. (10–30 min) Air fry 🥕 carrots, 🧅 onions, 🧄 garlic and 🫘 chickpeas in Ninja at 200°C for 18–20 minutes until tender and lightly caramelised.

4. (~32 min) Transfer air-fried veg and 🫘 chickpeas to a large oven tray. Pour the broccoli-sauce mixture over everything. Roast at 200°C for 13 minutes.

5. (~45 min) Add halved 🍒 cherry tomatoes. Roast final 10 minutes until sauce is bubbling and tomatoes have collapsed.

6. (~55 min) Squeeze 🍋 lemon juice over. Rest 3 minutes. Serve with cold 🥛 cottage cheese on the side — **do not stir into hot bake** (it splits).
